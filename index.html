Welcome to f(draw) ✦

  • Draw a curve and f(draw) finds its equation y = f(x). Click Copy to paste it into Desmos and get the same graph.
  • Each stroke must be a function of x: one-one or many-one is fine, but not one-many — if a vertical line x = c meets it twice, the fit won't match. Separate strokes are independent.
  • Draw at a steady pace. Very fast strokes record fewer points, so quick bumps and sharp peaks can get cut short.
  • f′ shows a curve's derivative, f⁻¹ its inverse, and ↔ extends it across the view. Hover a dot to see where curves cross.
  • The Smooth slider cleans up jittery strokes. Click a curve's dot to hide it, or its swatch to change its colour.
