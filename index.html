Welcome to f(draw) ✦

  • Draw slowly and smoothly — sharp rapid turns are hard to fit accurately
  • Each stroke must be a function of x: one-one or many-one is fine, but not one-many — if a vertical line x = c meets it twice, the fit won't match. Separate strokes are independent.
  • Use the Smooth slider to clean up jittery strokes
  • Scroll to zoom · Right-drag to pan · Click dot to toggle visibility
  • Click the color swatch to change a curve's colour
